Points for Attention

    All tickets in the Nema helpdesk instance since go-live (24 August 2026), incidents and service requests together. Ticket closure rate = (closed + resolved) ÷ all tickets raised in the period; target above 90% for AY 2026/27. First call resolution (FCR) = L1 tickets marked first-call resolved ÷ L1 tickets closed or resolved; L1 = Support Abu Dhabi, Support Al Ain and L1 Support groups (ADU, Liwa, ArcLight, Knowledge Group); target 60%. MTTR = average time from ticket logged to closed, for tickets closed in the period, counted in business hours (Monday to Friday, 9 AM – 6 PM UAE time; public holidays not excluded), with clock hours shown alongside. Open backlog = Open + On Hold. NEMA combines NEMA Holding and NEMA Helpdesk accounts. Salesforce notifications are automated system tickets, shown separately. Months are by date raised, UAE time; October is month to date.
